CVlens

Aesthetic ATS intelligence. Format, match, and analyze your resume in a clean, minimalist workspace designed for clarity.

With modern hiring heavily relying on automated ATS systems, highly qualified candidates are routinely filtered out simply due to unoptimized resume formatting or missing keywords. CVLens bridges this gap by offering an aesthetic, AI-driven workspace that reverse-engineers the ATS process. It cleanly restructures your resume, benchmarks it against target job descriptions, and extracts missing core terminology, ensuring your application gets the visibility it deserves.

How it works

  • 1. Upload Context: Start by uploading your PDF resume and pasting a target Job Description. This context is shared seamlessly across all modules.
  • 2. Format: Automatically restructure your resume into an ATS-friendly, clean document layout without losing critical content.
  • 3. Match & Analyze: Benchmark your qualifications against the job description to receive a quantitative score, uncover knowledge gaps, and extract core terminology.
  • 4. Generate: Use AI to reverse-engineer an ideal job description based on your current resume to discover roles that perfectly fit your background.
